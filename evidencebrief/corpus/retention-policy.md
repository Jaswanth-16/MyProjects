# Synthetic client-retention policy

Retention interventions require human approval before contacting a customer or changing a contract.

A model score indicates an estimated association in the available data; it does not prove that a support call caused churn.

Customer contact details must be removed from prompts. Only approved, non-confidential documents may be sent to an external model provider.

Analysts must document the data snapshot, target definition, train-test split, baseline and evaluation limitations before presenting results.
